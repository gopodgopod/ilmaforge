Privacy & cookies

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This page explains how ILMA Studio uses analytics on ilmaforge.com. You can use the website without allowing analytics.

Who operates this website

ILMA Studio operates ilmaforge.com. For questions about privacy or analytics, contact contact@ilmaforge.com.

Google Analytics

If you choose “Allow analytics”, this website uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to measure visits and understand how people find and use the site. It may process pages viewed, visit source or referrer, browser and device information, and an approximate location derived from the connection. Google may use cookies such as _ga to distinguish browsers.

Analytics is off until you make that choice. If you choose “Reject”, or do not make a choice, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. Advertising features and account-level Google Analytics data-sharing options are off. This Analytics property is linked to the ilmaforge.com property in Google Search Console, so search performance information such as queries and clicks can appear alongside Analytics reports for users who can access this Analytics property.

Your choice

Your choice is saved in this browser’s local storage so the site can remember it. You can change or withdraw it at any time using the button below. If you withdraw consent, the site stops sending further analytics events and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies for this site.

Google’s privacy information

Google’s handling of information is described in Google’s Privacy Policy. This page covers analytics on this website; it is not legal advice.
